The Free version includes weight logging, BMI gauge, BMI calculator page, list & calendar views, Apple Health, iCloud, CSV import/export, and full theming — with ads on the main screen and one user profile. Premium unlocks goals & milestones, voice entry, the photo journal & timelapse, up to 5 user profiles, and removes all ads.
Voice entry is a Premium feature. On the main screen, tap the microphone button and speak your weight — WeightTrack will automatically log it for you. No typing required.
Photo capture is a Premium feature. When adding a new weight entry, tap the camera icon to take or choose a photo. All your photos are collected in the Photo Journal, where you can view them as a timelapse to see your visual transformation over time.
On the history screen, tap the view toggle button at the top to switch between List and Calendar view. Calendar view highlights the days you've logged a weight — tap any date to see that entry. Both views are available in the free version.
Multiple user profiles are a Premium feature, supporting up to 5 separate profiles. To add a profile, go to Settings → Profiles and tap Add Profile. You can switch between profiles from the main screen — each profile has its own history, goals, and BMI.
The BMI Calculator page shows your current BMI alongside a breakdown of all weight ranges — Underweight, Normal, Overweight, and Obese — so you can see exactly where you sit and what your goals mean in context. It's available in both the free and premium versions.

Tips

Getting the Most from WeightTrack

01

Weigh at the same time

For the most accurate trends, weigh yourself at the same time each day — first thing in the morning works best.

02

Log consistently

Daily logging gives you the clearest picture of your progress. Even a plateau is useful data.

03

Focus on the trend

Day-to-day fluctuations are normal. Use the weekly and monthly chart views to see your real progress.

04

Set a realistic goal

Set a goal weight and let WeightTrack show your progress — milestone markers keep you motivated every step of the way.

05

Use the calendar view

Switch to calendar view to quickly spot which days you've logged. Consistency is easier to track when you can see the gaps at a glance.

06

Capture your progress

With Premium, take a photo with each entry. Looking back at a timelapse of your journey is one of the most motivating things you can do.
