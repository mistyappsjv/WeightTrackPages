WeightTrack for iPhone

Simple weight tracking, with style.

Log your weight, track your BMI, and visualise your journey with beautiful charts. Choose list or calendar view, set goals and milestones, capture photos with entries, use voice input, and manage up to 5 profiles — all in one clean iPhone app.

Progress

See your full journey at a glance.

A beautiful interactive chart shows your weight history over days, weeks, months, or your entire lifetime — with your goal line always in view.

  • Long-range history chart with scrollable timeline
  • Goal weight line and milestone markers on the chart
  • Daily change indicators — instantly spot trends
  • BMI gauge and calculator displayed on the main screen

Logging

Add an entry in seconds.

The quick-entry interface is designed for speed — type your weight, or just tap the microphone and speak it. Attach a photo to document your progress visually.

  • Tap +/- stepper or type your weight directly
  • Voice entry — speak your weight via the mic button (Premium)
  • Attach a photo to any weight entry (Premium)
  • Optional notes for each entry (mood, meals, etc.)

Personalisation

Built around your profile.

Set up your gender, age, height and goal weight so WeightTrack can calculate your BMI accurately and show you meaningful progress toward your target.

  • Accurate BMI calculated from your profile
  • Goal weight progress shown on dashboard & chart
  • Up to 5 separate user profiles (Premium)
  • Switch between Metric and Imperial at any time

Connectivity

Connected to your Apple ecosystem.

WeightTrack plays nicely with the tools you already use — sync weight entries to Apple Health, back up with iCloud, or move your data in and out with CSV.

  • Write weight entries to Apple Health automatically
  • iCloud sync to keep all your devices up to date
  • Export your full history as a CSV file
  • Import pre-existing weight data from a CSV

Views

Your history, your way.

Toggle between a clean scrollable list view and an interactive calendar view — browse your entries in whichever layout you prefer.

  • Calendar view highlights days you've logged at a glance
  • List view shows full entry details with swipe-to-edit actions
  • Tap any calendar date to jump straight to that entry
  • Available in both free and premium versions

Premium — Goals

Stay motivated with milestones.

Set a target weight and let WeightTrack celebrate your progress — milestone markers keep you motivated every step of the way.

  • Set a goal weight and track progress on the dashboard
  • Milestone markers displayed on your progress chart
  • Progress indicators keep you focused on the journey
  • Available exclusively in WeightTrack Premium

Premium — Photo Journal

Capture your transformation.

Take a photo each time you log your weight and watch your journey come alive in a beautiful timelapse photo journal — your progress, documented.

  • Attach a photo to any weight entry with one tap
  • View all your photos in the dedicated photo journal
  • Timelapse view shows your visual transformation over time
  • Available exclusively in WeightTrack Premium

Premium — Voice & Profiles

Log with your voice. Track the whole family.

Tap the microphone on the main screen to speak your weight instantly — no typing required. Create up to 5 separate profiles for the whole household.

  • Voice entry — just say your weight and it's logged
  • Up to 5 individual user profiles
  • Each profile has its own history, goals, and BMI
  • Switch between profiles in seconds from the main screen
