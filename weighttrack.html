Progress

See your full journey at a glance.

A beautiful interactive chart shows your weight history over days, weeks, months, or your entire lifetime — with your goal line always in view.

  • Long-range history chart with scrollable timeline
  • Goal weight line displayed directly on the chart
  • Daily change indicators — instantly spot trends
  • BMI displayed alongside every entry

Logging

Add an entry in seconds.

The quick-entry interface is designed for speed — tap the date, adjust your weight with the stepper or type it in, add an optional note, and you're done.

  • Tap +/- stepper or type your weight directly
  • Optional notes for each entry (mood, meals, etc.)
  • Edit or delete any past entry at any time
  • Supports dates in the past for catch-up entries

Personalisation

Built around your profile.

Set up your gender, age, height and goal weight so WeightTrack can calculate your BMI accurately and show you meaningful progress toward your target.

  • Accurate BMI calculated from your profile
  • Goal weight progress shown on dashboard & chart
  • Toggle daily change, BMI, and goal displays on or off
  • Switch between Metric and Imperial at any time

Connectivity

Connected to your Apple ecosystem.

WeightTrack plays nicely with the tools you already use — sync weight entries to Apple Health, back up with iCloud, or move your data in and out with CSV.

  • Write weight entries to Apple Health automatically
  • iCloud sync to keep all your devices up to date
  • Export your full history as a CSV file
  • Import pre-existing weight data from a CSV
